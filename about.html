About Hyper Packaging Solutions

A dedicated manufacturer and supplier of polythene packaging solutions, built on consistent quality, custom engineering, and dependable wholesale supply.

Our Background

Manufacturing Excellence in Polythene Packaging

Hyper Packaging Solutions was founded with a clear operational mission: to supply industries, retailers, food processors, garment exporters, and wholesalers with dependable, high-precision polythene packaging products.

We operate specialized blown film extrusion machinery, precision flexographic presses, rotogravure printing units, and automatic bag-making and sealing lines. From continuous rolls to finished custom-printed bags, every product is manufactured to exacting specifications of gauge, tensile strength, and optical clarity.

Whether your business requires heavy-duty industrial box liners, retail shopping bags, or high-barrier food pouches, our team works closely with you to engineer the right polymer formulation and deliver on schedule.

Our Purpose

Mission, Vision & Core Strengths

Guiding principles that define our commitment to quality manufacturing and responsive client partnerships.

Our Mission

To deliver consistently engineered, durable, and cost-effective polythene packaging solutions that protect our customers' products and elevate their brand presence across wholesale supply chains.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted manufacturing partner for flexible polythene packaging, recognized for dimensional accuracy, printing fidelity, and dependable on-time fulfillment.

Strict Quality Control

Every production batch undergoes gauge uniformity testing, dart impact resistance checks, seal strength verification, and ink rub-resistance testing before packing.

Operational Pillars

What Sets Our Facility Apart

Built from the ground up for industrial scalability, customized orders, and wholesale reliability.

Modern Extrusion

State-of-the-art blown film extruders with automatic gauge profile adjustment to prevent thin spots and gauge variation.

Dual Print Capabilities

Both flexographic and rotogravure printing in-house, giving you maximum flexibility for both quick batches and million-run jobs.

Recyclable Options

100% recyclable mono-material LDPE and HDPE options, plus post-industrial recycled blends (PIR) for eco-conscious procurement.

Direct Partnership

Direct collaboration with our manufacturing plant managers and technical sales specialists without middleman communication gaps.
