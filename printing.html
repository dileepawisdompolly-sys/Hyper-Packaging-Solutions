Two Distinct Printing Methods

Choosing between Flexographic and Gravure printing depends on your production volume, artwork complexity, budget, and speed requirements.

Short-To-Medium Runs • Rapid Turnaround

Flexographic (Flexo) Printing

Rotary relief printing using flexible photopolymer elastomer plates mounted on print cylinders.

Request Flexo Quote

Flexographic printing is the industrial workhorse for polythene film packaging. Ink is transferred from anilox rolls to raised surface plates, directly onto high-speed LDPE, HDPE, or PP webs. It excels at bold spot colors, typography, company logos, barcodes, and regulatory recycling symbols.

Key Advantages of Flexo:

Cost-Effective Tooling

Significantly lower initial plate setup costs compared to metal cylinder engraving.

Fast Lead Times

Photopolymer plates are produced rapidly, shortening pre-press lead time for urgent shipments.

Color Flexibility

Easily switch Pantone spot inks, metallic inks, and custom brand hues between batches.

High-Volume • Photorealistic Fidelity

Gravure (Rotogravure) Printing

Intaglio printing using micro-laser engraved copper cylinders plated with hard industrial chrome.

Request Gravure Quote

Gravure printing is the premier choice for large-scale packaging programs where uncompromising print perfection and photographic clarity are paramount. Cells etched into heavy metal cylinders hold ink that transfers under high pressure to BOPP, PP, or laminated pouches, delivering seamless gradients and deep ink density.

Key Advantages of Gravure:

Unmatched Image Quality

Photorealistic pictorials, smooth vignettes, fine micro-text, and zero plate-bounce artifacts.

Extreme Cylinder Durability

Cylinders endure millions of meters of continuous printing with zero degradation or wear.

Lowest Per-Unit Cost in Bulk

Once cylinders are engraved, high-speed runs deliver the lowest per-unit printing cost.

Workflow

Our Custom Design & Pre-Press Process

We ensure your artwork transitions accurately from digital vector file to printed polythene film.

1

Artwork Submission

Provide your design files in vector format (.AI, .PDF, or .EPS). Specify your required Pantone Solid Coated spot color references.

2

Pre-Press Review & Proofing

Our prepress technicians verify bleed allowances, trap lines, barcode scanning clearance, and send you a digital PDF approval proof.

3

Plate / Cylinder Making

Upon proof sign-off, high-precision photopolymer plates (Flexo) or electronic laser-engraved cylinders (Gravure) are created.

4

High-Speed Print & QA

Inline printing with electronic tension control, ink viscosity monitoring, and corona surface treatment for maximum ink adhesion.
