Precision In-House Printing

Our Printing Solutions

Enhance your packaging with crisp branding, regulatory information, and barcodes using our dual printing capabilities.

Cost-Effective & Fast

Flexographic (Flexo) Printing

Flexographic printing utilizes flexible photopolymer plates wrapped around rotating cylinders. Perfect for small-to-medium wholesale batches, quick turnarounds, and bold graphic branding.

Lower Plate Costs: Significantly faster setup and economical tooling for new designs.
Fast Turnaround: Agile plate production enables rapid manufacturing and quick repeat orders.
Up to 6 - 8 Colors: Crisp spot colors, sharp typography, logo branding, and standard barcodes.
Explore Flexo Details & Specs
Photorealistic & Long Runs

Gravure (Rotogravure) Printing

Gravure printing uses laser-engraved copper and chrome cylinders. Engineered for massive bulk quantities, photorealistic pictorial fidelity, and exact color repeatability across millions of units.

Ultra-High Graphic Resolution: Photorealistic gradients, fine details, and complex halftone designs.
Durable Cylinders: Engraved cylinders run millions of impressions without image degradation.
Up to 8 - 10 Colors: Continuous color density with full-color process printing (CMYK + spot Pantone).
Explore Gravure Details & Specs
