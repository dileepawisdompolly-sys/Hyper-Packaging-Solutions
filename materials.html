Packaging Polymers & Materials

Understanding the core polymer resins we extrude and convert: LDPE, HDPE, PP, BOPP, and co-extruded barrier films.

Polymer Science

Detailed Material Breakdown

Selecting the ideal polymer ensures optimal product protection, mechanical strength, visual appeal, and cost efficiency.

Polyethylene Family

LDPE (Low-Density Polyethylene)

Density: ~0.910 - 0.940 g/cm³ • The versatile champion of flexible packaging films.

Quote LDPE Packaging

LDPE is defined by its branched polymer chain structure, granting it exceptional elasticity, tear propagation resistance, and impact toughness. It forms strong, dependable hermetic heat seals even in demanding environments. Excellent for products with irregular edges or heavy load weights.

Optical Clarity
Translucent / Moderate Gloss
Flexibility & Feel
Soft, Supple & Highly Elastic
Puncture Resistance
Very High (Tear Resistant)
Moisture Barrier
High Hydrophobic Barrier
Typical Applications:

Industrial continuous reels, heavy-duty carton box liners, zip-lock bags, construction sheeting, agricultural films, and bulk hardware packaging.

Polyethylene Family

HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene)

Density: ~0.941 - 0.965 g/cm³ • High tensile strength, rigid structure, and crinkly texture.

Quote HDPE Packaging

HDPE features linear polymer chains with minimal branching, creating tightly packed molecular crystalline zones. This yields higher tensile strength per micron, making it possible to produce ultra-thin, lightweight bags that carry impressive weights without snapping.

Optical Clarity
Semi-Opaque / Matte Frosted
Flexibility & Feel
Stiff, Paper-like & Crinkly
Tensile Strength
Exceptional Load-Bearing
Moisture Barrier
Superior Water-Vapor Guard
Typical Applications:

Supermarket grocery T-shirt bags, retail punch handle bags, waste & trash can liners, butcher paper wraps, and thin protective interleaving sheets.

Polypropylene Family

PP (Polypropylene)

Density: ~0.900 - 0.910 g/cm³ • Superior optical clarity and high melting threshold.

Quote PP Packaging

Polypropylene is prized for its high surface luster and glass-like transparency. It is stiffer than LDPE and possesses higher thermal tolerance, preventing deformation under moderate warmth. Perfect for consumer products where the package contents must look crisp and appealing on retail display.

Optical Clarity
High Transparency & Gloss
Flexibility & Feel
Medium Rigidity, Smooth
Thermal Tolerance
Higher Melting Point (~160°C)
Chemical Resistance
Resistant to Fats & Acids
Typical Applications:

Garment display bags, shirts and textiles, bakery & confectionery bags, medical disposables, and retail consumer goods packaging.

Biaxially-Oriented Film

BOPP (Biaxially-Oriented Polypropylene)

Stretched in both machine and transverse directions • Maximum dimensional stability and pristine print surface.

Quote BOPP Packaging

BOPP film goes through a dual-axis mechanical stretching process that aligns its crystalline structure. This achieves ultra-crisp, crystal-clear optics, high dimensional stiffness, and unmatched barrier performance against moisture and odors. It serves as the gold standard substrate for gravure printing.

Optical Clarity
Crystal Clear (Glass-like)
Flexibility & Feel
Crisp, High Tensile Stiffness
Print Definition
Exceptional Ink Adhesion
Barrier Properties
High Moisture & Aroma Barrier
Typical Applications:

Greeting cards & stationery, snack food wrapping, self-adhesive peel-and-seal apparel bags, magazine wraps, and laminated pouches.

Advanced Formulations

For demanding industrial or high-barrier food packaging, we manufacture multi-layer co-extruded films and laminated substrate combinations (such as PET/LDPE, BOPP/CPP, or metalized films). These multi-layer barriers block oxygen ingress, UV rays, moisture, and light, dramatically extending product shelf life.
